About me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Instrument Science and Engineering, Southeast University, advised by Prof. Lifeng Zhu. I conduct research at the Institute of Robot Sensing and Control Technology and the Jiangsu Province Key Laboratory for Robot Perception and Control Technology.

I am currently exploring robot learning and vision–language–action (VLA) models with compliant control, with a particular interest in connecting task understanding to real-time physical interaction.

My research spans robot learning, medical robotics, and physical human–robot interaction. I study how robots can achieve precise motion, compliant contact, and effective collaboration in confined spaces and complex interaction environments. My work spans concentric-tube robot steering, shared control for surgical retraction, and visual–haptic feedback for robot-assisted bronchoscopy.

I welcome conversations and collaborations in robot learning, medical robotics, and human–robot interaction. Get in touch ↗
